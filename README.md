## What it covers

- Article volume
- Sentiment

| Data | Ask with | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| History and growth | a keyword: any keyword or phrase | News article mention volume |
| History and growth | a keyword: any keyword or phrase | News sentiment score (positive / negative) |
| Live board: Google News Top News | no keyword | Top news stories from Google News |

## How to ask

Pass any keyword for how often it is mentioned and whether coverage is positive or negative. Google News top stories need no keyword.

**Good to know:** This is volume, sentiment, and the top-stories list. It does not return the full text of articles.

## FAQ

**Does this score sentiment?**  
Yes. For a keyword you get mention volume and whether coverage is positive or negative.

**Is Google News search volume the same thing?**  
No. Google News search volume is on the Google Trends connector. This page is article mentions, sentiment, and top stories.

**Is the News connector free?**  
Yes. The free plan has 100 requests a month and includes every source. No credit card. Paid plans start at $19 a month for 1,000 requests.

**Which AI apps work with it?**  
Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, Claude Code, Grok, Muse, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Windsurf and any app that supports MCP connectors.
